=== Theoretical Interpretation (for LaTeX write-up) ===
Let C_k(x) be the BB prediction set under key k and C_cap^(K)(x)=intersection_{k=1}^K C_k(x).
As K increases, |C_cap^(K)(x)| is non-increasing by construction, so ambiguity should shrink.
Coverage of C_cap^(K) can decrease with K, but empirically often remains high due to correlated errors.
Bonferroni bound: P(y in C_cap^(K)) >= 1 - sum_{k=1}^K alpha_k, where alpha_k=1-coverage_k.
Independence estimate: P(y in C_cap^(K)) ~= prod_{k=1}^K (1-alpha_k).
When realized coverage is much higher than the independence estimate, key-wise misses are positively correlated.
This means many hard samples are shared across keys rather than missed independently.
Practical takeaway: increasing K can substantially reduce set size while preserving useful coverage.
